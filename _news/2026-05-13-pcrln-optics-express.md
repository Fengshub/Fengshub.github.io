Our paper, "Unrolled Richardson-Lucy deconvolution network with partially connected layers in computational microscopy," was published in *Optics Express* 34(10), 18676-18689 (2026). We introduce PC-RLN, an unrolled Richardson-Lucy network with learnable partially connected layers for computational microscopy with multi-cluster point spread functions. The work evaluates the method in Fourier light-field microscopy and microlens-array integrated microscopy. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.588318) or explore the [code and project details](https://github.com/Fengshub/Partially-Connected-Unrolled-RL-Network).
