Computational imaging researcher at the University of California, Davis.

Lakeside, Lake Tahoe

Misty boardwalk, Yellowstone National Park

University of California, Davis

I am a researcher working on computational imaging, lensless cameras, and machine-learning methods for 3D microscopy. My work combines optical design, physics-informed reconstruction, and deep learning to build compact imaging systems with large fields of view and practical computational requirements.

My research includes lensless 3D cameras, integrated fluorescence microscopy, learned reconstruction algorithms, and computational microscopy.

news

Jun 23, 2026 Our mini-review, “Photonics Breakthroughs 2025: Mask-Based Integrated Miniscope for Large-scale Volumetric Imaging,” was published in IEEE Photonics Journal, 18(4), article 3900408. The article highlights DeepInMiniscope and recent advances in mask-based integrated imaging and computational reconstruction. Read the paper or explore the DeepInMiniscope project.
May 13, 2026 Our paper, “Unrolled Richardson-Lucy deconvolution network with partially connected layers in computational microscopy,” was published in Optics Express 34(10), 18676-18689 (2026). We introduce PC-RLN, an unrolled Richardson-Lucy network with learnable partially connected layers for computational microscopy with multi-cluster point spread functions. The work evaluates the method in Fourier light-field microscopy and microlens-array integrated microscopy. Read the paper or explore the code and project details.
