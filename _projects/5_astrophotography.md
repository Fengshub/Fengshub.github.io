**Capture details:** Astro-Tech AT60ED 60 mm f/6; Canon T3i (astro-modified); iEXOS-100-02 mount; SVBONY SV165 30 mm guide scope and SV905C guide camera; SVBONY SV220 7 nm dual-band filter; 19 × 180 s + 20 × 240 s; Northern California, Bortle 7.
