GEOMScope combines a thin microlens array with geometrical-optics-based pixel back projection and background suppression for single-shot, large-field-of-view 3D lensless microscopy.

- [Code and optical design files](https://github.com/Fengshub/GEOMScope)
- [Laser & Photonics Reviews paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/lpor.202100072)
