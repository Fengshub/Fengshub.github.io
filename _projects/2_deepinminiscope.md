DeepInMiniscope is a compact, mask-based integrated fluorescence microscope with a physics-informed deep-learning reconstruction model for large-field-of-view 3D imaging. The system was demonstrated for neural activity imaging in awake mouse cortex.

- [Code and resources](https://github.com/Fengshub/DeepInMiniscope-Learned-Lensless-Miniscope)
- [Science Advances paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adr6687)
